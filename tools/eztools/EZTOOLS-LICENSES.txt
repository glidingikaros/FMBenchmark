upstream repository on 2026-10-08. The tarball holds framework-dependent builds of AmcacheParser,
AppCompatCacheParser, EvtxECmd (evtx), JLECmd, LECmd, MFTECmd, PECmd and RECmd (with RegistryPlugins),
made from the sources pinned in src/fmd/collection/tools/host/eztools-lock.json.
